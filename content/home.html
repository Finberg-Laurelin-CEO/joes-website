Introduction

I am Joe Finberg. I run Laurelin Technologies, where we build a containerized deuterium fusion reactor. I also built Frege, a shared memory for teams that run many AI agents.

I studied physics and philosophy at Columbia and Oxford, and I still publish on plasma physics and the foundations of time.

I also write poems, essays, and a novel, False Vacuum. This site collects that work. I put new writing in Posts.
